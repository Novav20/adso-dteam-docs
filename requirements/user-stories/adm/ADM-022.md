# ADM-022: Optimization Suggestions (PMO)

## Story Description

| Role | Action | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance Supervisor | I want to see automatic suggestions on maintenance frequency based on the equipment unit's failure history. | So that I can identify equipment being over-maintained and adjust the plan to reduce unnecessary costs. |

## Acceptance Criteria
| Scenario | Given (Context) | When (Action) | Then (Result) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Over-maintenance detection | Given an equipment unit has monthly preventive maintenance and zero registered failures in the last 12 months. | When the Supervisor accesses the PMO Optimization module. | Then the system displays a suggestion card: 'Equipment Unit [ID] shows high reliability. Suggested to decrease frequency to Bimonthly'. |
| Apply frequency change suggestion | Given the system displays a frequency change suggestion. | When the Supervisor clicks 'Apply Change'. | Then the associated Preventive Maintenance Plan is automatically updated with the new frequency, recording the approving user. |
