# VIS-011: Isolation Route (LOTO) and Active Lock Visualization

## Story Description

| Role | Action | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance Technician | I want to visualize the energy isolation route (LOTO) on the Digital Twin and have the system block the start of work if it detects active energy. | So that I can ensure the intervention is performed under Zero Energy conditions, preventing occupational accidents. |

## Acceptance Criteria
| Scenario | Given (Context) | When (Action) | Then (Result) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Isolation Point Visualization | Given the Technician has selected a Work Order on their device. | When they activate the 'View LOTO Route' function. | Then the Digital Twin highlights the energy route and the lockout points on the corresponding 2D/3D view. |
| Connection Validation | Given the LOTO connection lines are displayed. | When the Technician selects the suggested isolation point (e.g., Breaker). | Then the system displays the panel tag (Tag) that matches the physical reality. |
| Start Lock due to Detected Energy | Given the IoT simulator reports active energy levels above the safety threshold. | When the technician attempts to press 'Start Work'. | Then the system blocks the action in the interface and displays a clear hazard alert. |
| Zero Energy Validation | Given the IoT simulator confirms there is no active energy at the isolation points. | When the technician confirms the physical lockout on the checklist. | Then the system allows changing the WO status to 'In Execution'. |
| Lock due to Telemetry Loss (Fail-Safe) | Given the system cannot establish communication with the zone's IoT telemetry source. | When the technician attempts to start work or confirm isolation. | Then the system preventively blocks the operation, informing that Zero Energy cannot be guaranteed due to lack of real-time data. |
| Physical Try-Out Verification (Absence of Energy Test) | Given the sequential lockout checklist is completed and telemetry confirms zero energy at all cutoff points. | When the technician performs and confirms the local physical absence of energy test on the asset. | Then the system logs the Try-Out confirmation timestamp and unlocks the 'Start Work' active interlock control. |
| Emergency Re-energization Alert during Execution | Given the Work Order is in execution under an active LOTO isolation route. | When continuous background telemetry detects energy flow above the safety threshold at any cutoff point. | Then the system automatically forces the Work Order suspension, deploys an evacuation alarm on the tablet, and alerts HSEQ supervision. |
