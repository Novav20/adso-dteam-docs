# VIS-009: Hierarchical Navigation and Semantic Zoom

## Story Description

| Role | Action | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance Supervisor | I want to zoom into a specific area of the plant using the mouse scroll. | So that I can inspect the location of equipment units in dense areas in detail. |

## Acceptance Criteria
| Scenario | Given (Context) | When (Action) | Then (Result) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semantic Zoom (Breakdown) | Given the Supervisor is viewing the General Map (Level 1). | When they zoom into the 'Boilers' area. | Then the system smoothly transitions from the general area layout to displaying individual equipment unit icons and finally to the equipment's technical detail (Level 3). |
| Navigation Limits | Given the Supervisor has reached the maximum allowed detail level. | When they attempt to zoom in further. | Then the view locks smoothly via a damping animation to prevent loss of visual context. |
| Return to Context (Zoom Out) | Given the Supervisor is inspecting a specific equipment unit in maximum detail. | When they select 'General View' or perform a fast zoom out. | Then the camera zooms out until the entire process unit is shown, restoring peripheral vision of alarms and neighboring equipment statuses. |
