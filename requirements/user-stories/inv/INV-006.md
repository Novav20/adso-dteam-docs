# INV-006: Inventory Movement Management

## Story Description

| Role | Action | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse Manager | I want to technically record the receipts, issues, and returns of critical materials and spare parts. | So that I can maintain absolute integrity between the physical and digital inventory, guaranteeing the availability of materials for maintenance and the correct allocation of operational costs. |

## Acceptance Criteria
| Scenario | Given (Context) | When (Action) | Then (Result) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material Receipt (Inbound) | Given a critical spare parts order has been received at the storage facilities. | When the responsible staff formalizes the inbound by specifying the spare part, quantity received, and technical location in the warehouse. | Then the system increases the physical inventory levels, records the chronological transaction, and communicates the successful receipt confirmation. |
