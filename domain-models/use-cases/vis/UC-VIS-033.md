| **Description**             | Provides spatial navigation across plant functional locations using an HPHMI Level 2 canvas (combining Geometric and Semantic Zoom). Selecting an asset deploys an ISA-101 compliant Level 3 Equipment Task Faceplate disclosing operational health with analog limit context, active LOTO/PTW safety boundaries, actionable CMMS Work Orders, and live telemetry, with on-demand drill-down to Level 4 diagnostics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Preconditions**           | <ul><li>The Maintenance Technician is authenticated with authorized field execution permissions on the visualization module [[TR-006]].</li><li>The selected functional location (ISO 14224 Level 5) has an interactive schematic and spatial mapping configured in the system.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Main Flow**               | <ol><li>The technician accesses the Digital Twin module and selects a functional location (ISO 14224 Level 5) from the plant hierarchy.</li><li>The System retrieves the spatial configuration and renders the base Level 2 Process Area schematic using the neutral HPHMI palette [[TR-011]]. The canvas supports dual navigation combining <strong>Geometric Zoom</strong> (fluid SVG viewport scaling via mouse wheel or touch gestures) and <strong>Semantic Zoom</strong> (automatic information density switching based on scale thresholds: the zoomed-out view shows equipment silhouettes and critical alarms; the zoomed-in view reveals TagNumber identifiers, connection ports, and process variables).</li><li>The technician navigates the schematic (via panning and zooming) and selects the graphical representation of a specific equipment unit (ISO 14224 Level 6).</li><li>The System deploys a contextual <strong>Level 3 Equipment Task Faceplate</strong> containing:<ul><li>Equipment identification: TagNumber, technical description, operational status, and criticality rating.</li><li>Safety context summary: Active PTW number and verified LOTO isolation tags (e.g., locked boundary valves or breakers).</li><li>Actionable Work Orders: Active work orders displaying WO ID, Type (Preventive/Corrective), Status (e.g., In Progress, Ready), and Priority.</li></ul></li><li>The System requests a subscription to the asset's real-time physical telemetry channel [[TR-010]].</li><li>The Telemetry Service publishes the equipment's sensor readings.</li><li>The System processes the asynchronous update and refreshes the values in the Level 3 faceplate, presenting readings contextualized with normal operating ranges and alarm limits without interrupting canvas navigation.</li><li>(Optional) The technician triggers a drill-down action to consult Level 4 Diagnostics (complete equipment master catalog, technical datasheets, and full CMMS maintenance history).</li></ol> |
| **Alternate Flows**         | <p><strong>AF-001: Inspection of Asset without Spatial Mapping</strong></p><p>If the technician locates the asset through the textual search directory instead of the interactive schematic:</p><ol><li>The System checks if the asset has spatial metadata linked to the current schematic.</li><li>Upon detecting the absence of mapping, the System deploys the Level 3 faceplate in unmapped mode and disables the "Locate on Schematic" control.</li><li>The System displays an informative neutral badge: "Spatial Mapping Pending for this asset (ISO 14224 Level 6 unlinked)".</li></ol><br><p><strong>AF-002: Telemetry Signal Loss (Diagnostic State)</strong></p><p>In step 6, if the connection to the telemetry data stream is interrupted:</p><ol><li>The System detects the drop in the real-time subscription [[TR-010]].</li><li>The System freezes the last received readings, applies Priority 4 Diagnostic coding (neutral/magenta diagnostic indicator and warning symbol per ISA-101/HPHMI, avoiding process alarm colors), and displays the disconnection timestamp: "Stale Data. Connection Lost at [timestamp]".</li><li>The System automatically resumes dynamic updates once the link is restored.</li></ol>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Postconditions**          | <ul><li>The technician gains centralized Level 3 situational awareness of the asset's operational state, safety boundaries, task readiness, and telemetry with operational limits.</li><li>Direct navigation paths are enabled for on-demand Level 4 diagnostics or CMMS Work Order execution.</li><li>The base canvas is initialized and ready to support the overlay of advanced security layers (Work Permits and LOTO).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
