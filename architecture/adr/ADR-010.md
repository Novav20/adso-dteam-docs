# ADR-010: Decoupling Functional Location Slots from Serialized Equipment Units via AssetInstallation

## Context
In industrial plant maintenance compliant with **ISO 14224:2016** (Chapter 8.1 Taxonomy vs. Chapter 9.1 Physical Equipment) and **ISO 55001**, process positions (**Functional Locations**, Taxonomy Levels 1–5) carry permanent plant context—operational criticality ($1..10$), environmental exposure, SIMOPS spatial coordinates, and cumulative process failure history—while physical machinery (**Equipment Units**, Level 6) regularly rotates in and out of service for overhauls, repairs, and spare exchanges (*Rotable Asset Swaps*).

Coupling process attributes directly to serialized equipment records or modeling mounting with a single mutable foreign key alone destroys chronological installation lineage and breaks spatial/RIME continuity whenever a spare pump or motor swaps into an active slot.

## Decision
We will strictly separate permanent process slots (`tax.functional_locations`) from physical serialized machines (`tax.equipment_units`) and govern all mounting/dismounting transitions through an explicit chronological ledger entity **`AssetInstallation`** (`tax.asset_installations`):

1. **Invariant Slot Occupancy (1:1 Active Pairing):** A `FunctionalLocation` slot may host at most one active `EquipmentUnit` at any instant, and an `EquipmentUnit` may be mounted in at most one slot at a time. This invariant is enforced in PostgreSQL via partial unique indexes (`WHERE removed_at IS NULL`) on `tax.asset_installations (functional_location_id)` and `(equipment_unit_id)`.
2. **Warehouse `NULLS DISTINCT` Spares:** Unmounted units residing in storage (`lifecycle_status = 'IN_STORAGE'`) retain `functional_location_id IS NULL` on `tax.equipment_units` without violating active slot constraints.
3. **Atomic Rotable Rotation (`INV-025`):** Swapping machinery executes synchronously within a shared PostgreSQL transaction across bounded contexts (`TaxonomyDbContext`, `InventoryDbContext`, `SecurityDbContext` per [DT-ARQ-DB-DOC-001 §3.9](../DT-ARQ-DB-DOC-001.md#39-cross-dbcontext-atomic-transactions-idbcontexttransaction-sharing)), closing the outgoing `AssetInstallation` (`removed_at`) and opening the incoming row while chaining an immutable SHA-256 audit entry (`ADR-003`).

## Alternatives Considered
* **Direct Single Foreign Key Only (`equipment_units.functional_location_id` without history table):** Rejected. Overwriting the FK loses exact mount/dismount timestamps, operational run-hour baselines per slot, and ISO 14224 failure attribution across rotable exchanges.
* **Merging Functional Location and Equipment Unit into a Single Table:** Rejected. Violates ISO 14224 §8.1 separation between functional position and physical serial number, corrupting RIME criticality inheritance (`ADR-002`) and SIMOPS plot-plan bindings whenever a unit goes to repair.

## Consequences

### Positive
* Preserves unbroken process reliability metrics (MTBF/MTTR), RIME criticality scores, and Digital Twin plot-plan bindings on Level 5 slots across equipment swaps.
* Tracks complete serial-number custody across warehouse storage, overhaul repair loops, and multi-slot service histories.

### Negative
* Requires cross-table synchronization between `equipment_units.functional_location_id` and active `asset_installations` rows inside domain orchestrators (`ITaxonomyService` / `IInventoryService`).
