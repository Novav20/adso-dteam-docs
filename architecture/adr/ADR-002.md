# ADR-002: Strategy Pattern for RIME Prioritization Engine

## Context
Backlog management demands an objective prioritization in accordance with the ISO 55001 standard. The MVP standard defined a two-factor mathematical model, calculating the score by multiplying criticality by work class. However, in complex industrial operations, the maintenance department might adapt the RIME model incorporating economic factors, inventory impact, and maintainability. Statically hardcoding the two-factor formula couples the domain and hinders the commercial customization of the system.

## Decision
The **Strategy Pattern** is adopted to isolate the RIME calculation algorithm from the work request entities and backlog items.

The `IRimeCalculator` domain interface is defined. For the MVP, the standard strategy based on a 2-factor formula with a range of 1 to 100 will be injected. The architecture will remain open to dynamically register custom strategies through the Dependency Injection container without modifying controllers or entities.

## Alternatives Considered
* **Calculation embedded in the `WorkRequest` entity:** Rejected. Violates the Open/Closed Principle and forces modifying the aggregate root every time an industrial client requests to change the weights of the RIME matrix.
* **Calculation in database via stored procedures:** Rejected. Causes domain logic to leak into the persistence layer.

## Consequences

### Positive
* Allows the MVP to operate deterministically and independently of the completeness state of the inventory module.
* Satisfies the commercial extensibility requirement by allowing custom risk matrices.

### Negative
* Adds an abstraction layer to the backlog intake flow.
* KPI reports must be designed to be tolerant of different priority scales according to the active strategy.

### Risks and Technical Debt
* The implementation of an extended strategy will depend on the performance of cross-queries with the real-time inventory module.
